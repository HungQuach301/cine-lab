=== [[:File:Air Raid Damage in Britain during the Second World War HU36220A.jpg]] ===
Sadly, while this is a wonderful photograph, it's not public domain as a time-expired UK government work (which would be fifty years since creation). It was taken by Herbert Mason, a commercial photographer for the ''Daily Mail'', and published by them; the normal copyright regime would presumably apply and this would remain in copyright unless we can show otherwise.

Extensive details on the background of the image are at [[:en:St Paul's Survives]], and note [[Commons:Deletion requests/Image:StPaulBlitz.jpg|a 2008 deletion request]] for a copy of the same image.
