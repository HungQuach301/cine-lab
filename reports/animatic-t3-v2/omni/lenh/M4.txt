Night of 13 to 14 April 1781 in the old town of Gibraltar. Eye-level camera in a narrow stone street, steady and slow. The street is lit by the orange glow of house fires burning further up the street, out of focus in the distance; sparks and smoke rise far behind. Townspeople in 18th-century civilian clothes (coats, shawls, caps), some carrying bundles and holding children's hands, hurry down the street toward the camera and past it, fleeing south away from the fire. Nobody is inside the flames or smoke; the fire stays in the background.
