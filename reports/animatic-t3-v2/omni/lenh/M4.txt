Night of 13 to 14 April 1781 in the old town of Gibraltar. Camera at rooftop height above a narrow stone street, steady, very slow push forward. Several houses further up the street are burning; the orange glow lights the walls and tiled roofs, dark smoke rises into the night sky. Down in the street below, small groups of townspeople in 18th-century civilian clothes (coats, shawls, caps), some carrying bundles and leading children, hurry along the street away from the fire, toward the camera and out of frame. Nobody is inside the flames or smoke; the fire stays in the middle distance.
