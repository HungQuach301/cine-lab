August 1704, the sandy isthmus north of the Rock of Gibraltar, summer midday light. Eye-level camera beside a dusty sand track, slow lateral tracking. A family of townspeople in early 18th-century Spanish civilian clothes (long coats, waistcoats, broad-brimmed hats, women in long skirts, shawls and headscarves, children) walk away from the town carrying bundles, baskets and a small wooden chest, one leading a donkey loaded with belongings; other families follow along the track. The large limestone Rock stays behind them in the background. Dust, heat, sad and quiet mood.
