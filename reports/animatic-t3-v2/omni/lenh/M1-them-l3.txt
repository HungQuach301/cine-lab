Only ONE small open rowing boat with oars, no mast and no sail; no other vessel anywhere. No flagpole, no flag, no pennant, no pole or mast of any kind anywhere in the frame, including on top of the walls. The top of the walls is a plain stone parapet: no sentry box, no turret, no doorway, no ladder on the walls. The quay edge is old rough stone: no metal ladders, no iron rings, no bollards. The reddish round shapes stacked against the wall are the ends of wooden barrels lying on their sides.
