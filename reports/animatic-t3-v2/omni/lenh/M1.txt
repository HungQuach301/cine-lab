Gibraltar harbour, January 1780, winter sunshine. Eye-level camera on the old stone quay, very slow gentle push-in. A small wooden ship's rowing boat with oarsmen comes alongside the quay; sailors in short dark-blue jackets, loose white canvas trousers and low round hats hand up wooden barrels and crates; labourers in shirts and waistcoats carry them on their shoulders up the quay and add them to a pile of barrels and crates that grows bigger; a few soldiers in red coats, white waistcoats and black tricorn hats help and watch. Calm water, gulls, light breeze.
