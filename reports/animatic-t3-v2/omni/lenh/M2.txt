Gibraltar, January 1780, a hungry winter of the siege. Eye-level camera on a rocky limestone hillside with dry grass, thistles and low palmetto bushes; very slow drift. A thin, poorly dressed man and woman in worn 18th-century civilian clothes (wool coat, shawl, plain cap) bend down and pick wild plants (thistles, dandelions, wild leeks) by hand and put them in a cloth bag or basket; wind moves their clothes and the grass. Far away in the background, faint grey smoke drifts in the sky. Quiet, sombre mood, overcast soft light.
