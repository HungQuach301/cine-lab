**Subject:** Commercial rights for a Voice Design voice used in a monetised YouTube series and sold films

Hello ElevenLabs team,

I run a small independent studio producing a narrated, animated history documentary series in English. We are on a paid plan (Creator) and have chosen a voice we created ourselves with **Voice Design** as the narrator for the whole series:

- Voice name in My Voices: `CINE-CastingGiong-A2-v3`
- Voice ID: `VDLncRBHDjLHBqLhQ1Qt` (created 18 October 2026 from a Voice Design preview, model `eleven_multilingual_ttv_v2`)
- Account / workspace: [...]

The narration will be generated with Text to Speech (`eleven_multilingual_v2` or `eleven_v4`) and published (1) on YouTube with monetisation enabled, and (2) as films we sell directly (download / licensing to buyers). We plan to use the same voice for many episodes over several years. Before we commit, we would be grateful for a written answer to the following questions.

**1. Long-term commercial use.** Your help centre says:

> "All paid plans include a commercial license, provided you're not using Beta Services."
>
> "Content generated during a paid subscription can be used commercially, and indefinitely, subject to our Service-Specific Terms."

Can you confirm that audio generated with a Voice Design voice while on a paid plan may be used commercially and indefinitely, including in monetised YouTube videos and in films that we sell or license to third parties, also after our subscription ends?

**2. Is Voice Design a "Beta Service"?** Your documentation says:

> "Note: Voice Design is still experimental."

and the Beta Services Addendum defines Beta Services as services "designated as alpha, beta, pilot, preview, or similar designation", and says:

> "You agree not to (and agree not to permit any third party to): (a) use the Beta Services for any commercial purposes or in any production environment;"

Does the "experimental" label mean that Voice Design, or the voices it creates, or Text to Speech output generated with those voices, falls under the Beta Services Addendum? If not, could you confirm that the commercial licence in question 1 applies without restriction?

**3. Who holds rights in the designed voice itself.** Your Terms of Use say:

> "Except as expressly set forth herein, as between you and ElevenLabs, you retain all rights in and to your Output."

> "Output may be generated by, but does not include, ElevenLabs’ foundational and other artificial intelligence voice models (the "Voice Models") [...]"

and define a User Voice Model as a voice model "sounding like your voice or a voice you are authorized to share with us". A Voice Design voice is neither. (a) Is a Voice Design voice treated as a User Voice Model, as one of ElevenLabs' Voice Models, or as something else? (b) Do we have any exclusivity: can ElevenLabs or another user generate or obtain the same or a confusingly similar voice? (c) Is there anything that would stop us from using the voice as the recognisable narrator "brand" of our series?

**4. Removal or change.** Your documentation says designed voices are "only accessible through your account" and that "Deleting a voice cannot be undone". (a) Under what circumstances could ElevenLabs remove, disable or alter a Voice Design voice in our account (for example model deprecation, policy changes, or moderation)? (b) Would we receive notice, and how long? (c) If a voice or the model behind it is retired, does audio we already generated remain usable commercially? (d) Will the voice keep sounding the same when used with future TTS models, or should we expect changes?

**5. Attribution and disclosure.** Is any attribution to ElevenLabs required for paid-plan output used this way? Do you recommend or require any AI-voice disclosure on YouTube or in sold films beyond what the platforms themselves require?

Thank you. A short written confirmation for each point would let us keep it with our rights records for the series.
