## 2. Đoạn trích nguyên văn
- NASA1: "It was a job title, designating someone who performed mathematical equations and calculations by hand." "In 1935, Virginia Tucker… joined four other women in Langley's first 'computer pool.'" (lời: "five women").
- NASA1: "A 1942 report supplied further statistics on the 75 female computers LMAL had then: 'A good number of the computers are former high school teachers.'"
- NASA1: "By 1946… Tucker presided over a vastly expanded department that had trained about 400 women and placed them in sections across the facility."
- NASA1: "Initially grouped in a segregated section, the 'West Area Computers'…"; "segregated dining and bathroom facilities".
- NASA1: "…paid much better than the majority of jobs available to women in the 1940s and 1950s." "…doing more in a morning than an engineer alone could finish in a day." "…took on calculating work that had originally been done by the engineers themselves" / "essential but time-consuming work".
- NASA1: "It also provided an entry for women into the field of aeronautical research at a time when most simply were not being hired as engineers".
- NASA1: "The Bell Electronic Computer, a huge machine acquired by the NACA in 1947, had its own computing group" (lời: "the agency"). "With the introduction of the first electronic computers, human computers took on programming duties as well."
- NASA2: "human computers were a talented team of women who went on to become some of the earliest computer programmers"; "One of the human computers' main tasks was computing the planned trajectories"; "NASA launched the JPL-built Explorer 1, which became the first U.S. … satellite in 1958"; "While machine computers were capable of some speedy calculations, they weren't nearly as reliable or efficient as humans."
- NASA3: "one of several women who worked their way out of the computer pool and into actual engineering jobs — something that began happening with increasing frequency through the 1950s and into the 1960s."
- OOH90 tr. 215: "Computer programmers held 519,000 jobs in 1988." tr. 216: "AI principles and languages will increasingly be used in the years ahead, becoming just additional tools available to programmers." "Employment, however, is not expected to grow as rapidly as in the past as improved software and programming techniques simplify or eliminate some programming tasks." "Competition has increased for entry level positions".
- MLR25 tr. 2: "Software developers can use GenAI to develop, test, and document code"; "The effects of AI proliferation on this occupation are highly uncertain"; "increased productivity from the use of AI may lower prices and increase demand for software products"; "who may be needed to develop AI-based business solutions and maintain AI systems"; "increase 17.9 percent between 2023 and 2033".
- CAN tr. 1–2: "employment of young workers (ages 22–25) in AI-exposed jobs now stands 19% below where it would be had it kept pace with that of their less-exposed peers. Experienced workers show no comparable gap"; "employment of 22–25-year-olds in the two most exposed quintiles fell about 11% between November 2022 and June 2026, while… the three least-exposed quintiles grew about 10%"; "(4) it operates primarily through reduced hiring rather than increased separations"; "exclude firms in the technology sector, account for occupational interest rate exposure, or consider effects of work-from-home"; ví dụ nghề: "software development and customer service".
