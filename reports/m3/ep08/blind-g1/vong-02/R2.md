1. MAIN MESSAGE: Machine translation was long predicted to replace translators but arrived slowly; the profession has not vanished but shifted (to post-editing, remote and video work), though growth is slowed and the future ("yet") is open.

2. TONE: (b). Repeated "the work moved" framing and the lamplighter closing ("the flame keeps changing hands"). Segment 14 and the "cannot yet" note add a caution, so it is mostly (b) with some unease.

3. HOOK: Partly. The question is "what happened to the people who carry words between languages?" Closest answer is [15]: "the profession did not disappear. The work moved..." and then it reopens: "which part of the work stays with people, and for how long?"

9. TOP 3 FIXES:

2. Trim [03]-[08] institutional detail, and anchor dates with clear signposts ("in 1966...") so the 1954/1962/1966 chain is easy to follow.
