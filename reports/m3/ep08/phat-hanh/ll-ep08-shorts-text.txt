S2 — 1954: a machine that translated 250 words
On January 7, 1954, a Georgetown team and IBM showed a computer translating Russian sentences in New York. It knew only 250 words and six rules of grammar, and the forecasts said a translating machine could be ready within a few years. Full film on the channel.
Source: Hutchins, "The first public demonstration of machine translation: the Georgetown-IBM system, 7th January 1954". #history #translation #computers
