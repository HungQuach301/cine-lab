What about the claims desk itself? The computer reached it too: companies used it to keep reserves for unpaid claims and then to process the claims. But the BLS noticed something. On claims, the computer was intended to improve the company's loss record, rather than to save clerical man-hours. Field offices could send an approved claim amount to head office by wire, and a machine printed the check. The judgment, deciding what a claim was worth, stayed with people.
