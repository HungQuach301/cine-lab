How widely is this used? Hard numbers are scarce. The BLS's January 2026 review put it more gently: adjusters can use AI tools to assess photographs of property damage, which improves efficiency in generating payout estimates. In a survey by state insurance regulators, released in 2022, eighty-eight percent of one hundred ninety-three auto insurers said they use, plan to use, or plan to explore AI and machine learning. Accident image analysis was one of the claims uses. That counts plans as well as practice, so it tells us about intent, not about how many claims a machine already prices.
