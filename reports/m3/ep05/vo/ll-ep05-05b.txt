By the late 1980s, the computer had reached the adjuster's desk too. The government's 1990 handbook of occupations described claim representatives who kept records of clients and claims on computer terminals, and a growing number who carried portable lap-top computers on assignment. Material damage adjusters, it said, inspect automobile damage and use the latest computerized estimating equipment to prepare estimates of the damage. The machine now did the arithmetic of a repair. But a person still walked around the car, looked at the dent, and decided what it was worth.
