For one small job, the change is sharper. Auto damage appraisers look at wrecked cars and estimate repair costs. There were about thirteen thousand five hundred of them in 2025. The BLS projects about twelve thousand four hundred by 2035: down nine percent, almost twice the decline for adjusters. In its 2025 article on AI, the BLS described the shift: once photographs are taken, the analysis and initial payout estimates, traditionally prepared by an adjuster, can be autogenerated by AI.
