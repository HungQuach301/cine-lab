Why? In a 2025 article, BLS economists explained how they factor in artificial intelligence. They expect it to mainly affect jobs whose core tasks can be most easily replicated by generative AI in its current form. Customer service was one of their examples. In that earlier projection, for 2023 to 2033, these jobs were set to decline five percent. Medical transcriptionists, whose work also turns spoken words into records, were set to decline four point seven percent.
