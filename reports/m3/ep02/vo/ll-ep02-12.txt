If the operators' story is any guide, the question is not only how many jobs. It is who carries the cost. In the 1920s and 30s, the people already in the job paid the most. The next generation found other doors. Today, the early signals point the other way. AI helps newcomers most on the job, yet fewer young people are getting through the door. It is too soon to know if that will last. It depends on things no chart can show yet: how fast the change comes, and what new work grows around it. That is what we will be watching.
