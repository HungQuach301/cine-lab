What does AI actually do at the help desk? Economists Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li and Lindsey Raymond studied five thousand one hundred seventy-nine support agents at one large software company, most of them working in the Philippines. With an AI assistant, agents resolved fourteen percent more issues per hour on average. For newer and less-skilled agents, the gain was thirty-four percent. For the most experienced, it was minimal. New agents with two months on the job performed like agents with six months, without the tool. The authors are careful: the study cannot show what happens to jobs overall. Firms might hire more newcomers, or need fewer of them.
