So here is the comparison, on the same scale: change over ten years. Between 1930 and 1940, as the dial spread, the number of telephone-industry operators fell about sixteen percent. For customer service representatives, the projected decline from 2025 to 2035 is about five percent. Not the same technology. The same pattern: a repetitive human task, taken over by an automated system. But the differences matter. Customer service is about fifteen times larger. AI reaches into office work far beyond the phone. And one of these numbers is history. The other is only a forecast.
