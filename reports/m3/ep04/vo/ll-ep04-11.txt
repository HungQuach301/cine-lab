So here is the comparison, on the same scale: one source, one period, 2025 to 2035. Word processors and typists: down thirty-four percent. Data entry keyers: down twenty-five point five percent. Secretaries and administrative assistants: down about two percent. All jobs together: up three point five percent. Not the same technology as the typewriter. The same pattern: a repetitive human task, taken over by an automated system. The more a job is pure typing, the steeper the projected fall. The more it is organizing, judging and dealing with people, the smaller. The typewriter created the pool; the computer and AI are taking the typing away from it. And all four of these numbers are forecasts, not counts.
