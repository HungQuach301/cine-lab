And the pool kept filling. After the war, offices grew, paperwork grew, and so did the people who typed it. The census changed its job categories again in 1970, so the next decade is drawn as its own line. On the 1970 basis, stenographers, typists, and secretaries numbered about two point three million in 1960, and about three point nine million in 1970. That is up sixty-nine percent in ten years. At its peak, the typing pool was one of the largest workforces in the country.
