What does AI do to writing work itself? In a 2023 experiment, MIT economists Shakked Noy and Whitney Zhang gave four hundred forty-four college-educated professionals short writing tasks: press releases, short reports, delicate emails. Half of them, chosen at random, could use ChatGPT. Those with the tool finished about thirty-seven percent faster, ten minutes off an average of twenty-seven, and graders rated their work higher. The weakest writers gained the most. In the authors' words, the tool substantially compresses the productivity distribution. The study measured tasks, not jobs. And these were professionals writing for themselves, not typists.
