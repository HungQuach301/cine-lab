The clearest case is the one closest to the old stenographer, who turned spoken words into typed pages. Medical transcriptionists do that work today, for doctors. In its January 2026 review, the BLS projected their employment to decline four point nine percent from 2024 to 2034, and it named the reason: AI technology can recognize speech and transcribe audio. That comes from an earlier projection cycle than the other numbers, and it is a forecast, not the end of the job.
