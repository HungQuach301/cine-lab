Today, the job title that grew out of the typing pool, word processors and typists, is one of the fastest-declining occupations on the BLS list. It is projected to shrink from about forty thousand jobs in 2025 to about twenty-six thousand in 2035. That is down thirty-four percent. Data entry keyers, the people who type information into systems, are projected to fall twenty-five point five percent, from about one hundred thirty-two thousand to about ninety-eight thousand. Both are projections, not counts.
