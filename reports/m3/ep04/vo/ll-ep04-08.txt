The largest descendant of the typing pool is much bigger. In 2025, there were about three point five million secretaries and administrative assistants. The BLS projects a decline of about two percent by 2035, to about three point four million. Its explanation is direct: technology, including artificial intelligence systems and digital tools, enables staff in many organizations to prepare their own documents without the help of secretaries. The boss who once dictated a letter now writes it, with a machine helping.
