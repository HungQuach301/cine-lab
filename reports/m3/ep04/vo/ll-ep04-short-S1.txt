In 1900, the U.S. census counted about one hundred thirty-four thousand stenographers, typists, and secretaries. By 1970, on a newer census basis, about three point nine million. Most of them were women. The typewriter built one of the largest workforces in America. Today, the job title word processors and typists is projected to shrink thirty-four percent by 2035.
