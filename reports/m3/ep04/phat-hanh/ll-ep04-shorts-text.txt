S1 — Stenographers, typists & secretaries: 134,000 to 3.9 million
In 1900 the U.S. census counted about 134,000 stenographers, typists and secretaries; by 1970, on a newer census basis, about 3.9 million. Today word processors and typists are projected to shrink 34.4% by 2035. Full film: "The Typewriter Opened a Door for Millions of Women. Who Gets the Next One?" on the channel.
Source: Historical Statistics of the U.S. (Census, 1975); BLS Employment Projections 2025–35. #history #jobs #typewriter

S2 — The job AI names directly: medical transcription
Medical transcriptionists turn spoken words into typed records. BLS projected a 4.9% decline for 2024–34 "because AI technology can recognize speech and transcribe audio." A projection, not a count. Full film on the channel.
Source: BLS, Monthly Labor Review, January 2026. #AI #jobs

S3 — 37% faster with AI — but not typists
In a 2023 MIT experiment, 444 professionals finished short writing tasks about 37% faster with ChatGPT. The study measured tasks, not jobs. Full film on the channel.
Source: Noy & Zhang, MIT working paper (2023). #AI #writing
