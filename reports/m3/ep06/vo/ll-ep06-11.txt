Who made this: a hand, or a machine? For most of a century, the answer was a hand: first the hand that set the type, then the hand that drew the page. If the machine makes the first drafts, the BLS expects designers to shift their focus to the later stages of the work. What is left is the hand that decides: which draft, what to fix, and what is good enough to print. Whether deciding is enough paid work for the people who once drew every line by hand is something no projection can settle yet.
