So here is the comparison, with a caution. We will not set these numbers side by side: one is a count from 1988, one is a forecast, and one is a single platform's job posts. What we can compare is the pattern. Then, the computer took over setting the type, and the designer took the tool. Today, software drafts the first image, and the designer is the one handed the tool again. Not the same technology. The same pattern: a repetitive human task, taken over by an automated system. But the differences matter. Back then, the machine took the keystrokes, and there came to be more designers. This time, it reaches for the drawing itself, and the BLS projects fewer designers. So far, that is a forecast and a drop in job posts, not yet a count of jobs lost.
