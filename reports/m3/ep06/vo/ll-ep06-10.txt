So here is the comparison, with a caution. We will not set these numbers side by side: one is a count from 1988, one is a forecast, and one is a single platform's job posts. What we can compare is the pattern. Then, the computer took over setting the type, and designers began doing that work themselves, on screen. Today, software can draft the first image, and designers use it to reach a first draft much more quickly. Not the same technology. The same pattern: a repetitive human task, taken over by an automated system. But the differences matter. Back then, the machine took the keystrokes, and there came to be more designers. This time, it reaches for the drawing itself, and the BLS projects fewer designers. So far, that is a forecast and a drop in job posts, not yet a count of jobs lost.
