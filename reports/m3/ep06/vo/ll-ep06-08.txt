In January 2026, a BLS review described what generative AI does for graphic designers: it helps them explore ideas and create first drafts much more quickly, and automate repetitive tasks such as prototyping. The newest projections, for 2025 to 2035, go further. Automated design tools, such as artificial intelligence, are projected to make graphic designers more productive and reduce the need for these workers. Graphic designers held about two hundred fifty-three thousand jobs in 2025. The BLS projects about two hundred forty-nine thousand by 2035: a decline of one point seven percent, while all jobs together grow three point five percent.
