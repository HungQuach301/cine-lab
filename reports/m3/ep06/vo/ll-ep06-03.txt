What replaced it was cold type. Text was keyed into a computer that was programmed to hyphenate, space, and create columns of text, and a machine printed it onto photographic paper. Paste-up artists cut those columns apart and arranged them, with the illustrations, on a board. Increasingly, the BLS noted, the author did the keyboarding before the job was even sent out. Then came electronic pagination: a whole newspaper page, made up on a screen, eliminating the role of paste-up artists.
