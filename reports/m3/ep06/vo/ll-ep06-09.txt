Outside the forecasts, one study looked at a large online freelancing platform. After image generators such as Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and DALL-E 2 were released in 2022, posts for image creation work, graphic design and 3D modeling, fell about seventeen percent within a year, compared with manual jobs. The authors note the drop was larger than the platform's usual seasonal swings. Still, that is one platform, and it counts job posts, not jobs. But it is where a freelancer feels a change first: in the number of clients asking.
