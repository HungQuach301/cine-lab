Can the BLS see a change like this coming? Once, it did. In the early 2000s, digital cameras were replacing film, and the BLS projected that photographic process workers, the people who developed film, would decline. They fell by nearly nine in ten, to only nine thousand two hundred by 2023. But for a brand-new technology like generative AI, the BLS changes its projections only when its research provides clear evidence.
