Can the BLS see a change like this coming? Once, it did. In the early 2000s, digital cameras were replacing film, and the BLS projected that photographic process workers, the people who developed film, would decline. They fell by nearly nine in ten, to only nine thousand two hundred by 2023. That change was easy to see coming: the digital camera improved on a familiar tool, and its path was clear. The tools now reaching designers are different. For a brand-new technology like generative AI, the BLS changes its projections only when its research provides clear evidence.
