So here is the comparison, on the same scale: one source, one period, 2025 to 2035. Bank tellers: down about thirteen percent. Loan officers: up about one percent. All jobs together: up three point five percent. Not the same technology as the ATM. The same pattern: a repetitive human task, taken over by an automated system. The ATM took over the cash. Software now takes on more of the paperwork and the scoring. But the differences matter. Back then, cheaper branches meant more branches, and more tellers. Now the BLS expects fewer branches. And all three of these numbers are forecasts, not counts.
