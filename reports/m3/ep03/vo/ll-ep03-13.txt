The teller's story is the more hopeful kind. A machine took over the most repetitive task, and for decades the job grew anyway, because people did more of what the machine could not: advice, trust, a face at the window. But that came with a condition. Cheaper branches meant more branches. That condition may not hold now. With AI, it is an open question. It depends on whether banks create new work faster than software takes the old work away. That is what we will be watching.
