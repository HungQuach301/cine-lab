The teller's story is the hopeful one in this series. A machine took over the most repetitive task, and for decades the job grew anyway, because people did more of what the machine could not: advice, trust, a face at the window. Whether that happens again with AI is an open question. It depends on whether banks find new work for people faster than software takes the old work away. That is what we will be watching.
