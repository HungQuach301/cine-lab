Behind the loan officer sits the credit analyst, who studies the numbers behind a loan. In a 2025 article on artificial intelligence, BLS economists wrote that AI can synthesize large amounts of data and reach big-picture conclusions, and that these tasks are the essence of a credit rating. In that article's projection, an earlier one for 2023 to 2033, credit analyst jobs were set to decline three point nine percent. It belongs to a different projection cycle from the other numbers.
