Ask someone who has worked in banking, and the line looks like this. Machines took the routine: cash, statements, transfers. Models now score a loan in seconds. But a model answers one question — how risky is this? People still answer another — should we lend, and on what terms? And sometimes the most important thing a teller does is notice that something is wrong: a customer, nervous, sending money to a stranger. The machine processes the request. A person asks why.
