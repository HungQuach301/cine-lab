S1 — Software that drafts the estimate for a wrecked car
Auto damage appraisers estimate what it costs to fix a wrecked car. BLS projects their jobs to fall about 9% by 2035: "computer software can evaluate photographs of damaged property and calculate an estimated claim amount." A projection, not a count. Full film: "In 1966, Computers Couldn't Judge an Insurance Claim. Now Software Prices the Wreck" on the channel.
Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook (2025–35). #jobs #AI #insurance

S2 — 1966: the computer couldn't judge. 2026?
In 1966 the BLS found routine record keeping declined in two-thirds of insurance companies, but few people were laid off, and judgment stayed with people. Today BLS says AI can produce the initial payout estimate from photographs. Full film on the channel.
Source: BLS Bulletin 1468 (1966); Monthly Labor Review, February 2025. #history #AI #insurance

S3 — 88% of auto insurers — use, plan, or explore AI
In a 2022 survey by state insurance regulators, 88% of 193 auto insurers said they use, plan to use, or plan to explore AI and machine learning. That counts plans as well as practice. Full film on the channel.
Source: NAIC (2022). #AI #insurance
