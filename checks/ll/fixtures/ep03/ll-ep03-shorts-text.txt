S1 — The ATM didn't kill the bank teller
When cash machines spread, everyone expected bank tellers to disappear. They didn't: an average urban branch needed fewer tellers (20 → 13, 1988–2004), but banks opened more branches. Full film: "The ATM Didn't Kill the Bank Teller. Will AI?" on the channel.
Source: J. Bessen, IMF Finance & Development, March 2015. #history #jobs #banking

S2 — Bank tellers: 13% fewer by 2035?
About 339,200 Americans worked as bank tellers in 2025. BLS projects about 294,500 by 2035, a decline of 13%. A projection, not a count. Full film on the channel.
Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook (2025–35). #jobs #AI #banking

S3 — AI and the essence of a credit rating
BLS economists wrote that AI can synthesize large amounts of data and reach big-picture conclusions, "the essence of a credit rating". Credit analysts: projected −3.9% (2023–33). Full film on the channel.
Source: Monthly Labor Review, February 2025. #AI #jobs #credit
