How this film was made: sources, estimates, AI

Built from British and Spanish records and period maps. Where the two sides' numbers differ, the film gives both and says who counted. Estimated: the 1782 ruins, the siege camps' tents, everyday life on the isthmus, building heights, the 1782 coastline and borders; troops, crowds and ships are illustrative in number and layout. Four short close-up scenes are AI-generated illustrations (Google Gemini) and add no facts. The narration is a synthetic voice. Facts as of 18 October 2026.

Spotted an error? Reply with your source (title and page) and we'll check it. Corrections go in this comment.
