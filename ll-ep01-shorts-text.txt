TITLE
London Still Has Lamplighters. Five of Them. #shorts

DESCRIPTION
London still has gas street lamps: in Covent Garden, along the Mall, outside palaces and theatres. British Gas says its team looked after about 1,100 of them in 2023. That team is five people.

Source: British Gas, The Source, "Celebrating the coronation of King Charles III" (2023).

Which American Jobs Are Shrinking Fastest? #shorts

DESCRIPTION
The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projects which jobs will shrink fastest between 2025 and 2035. Word processors and typists: down 34%. Projections are forecasts, not verdicts.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employment Projections 2025–2035, Table 1.5.

TITLE
One Job in 270: The Elevator Operator #shorts

DESCRIPTION
About 13,000 elevator operators in 1900, nearly 100,000 by 1950, 37,000 by 1970. Economist James Bessen followed about 270 occupations from 1950 to 2010: only one disappeared mainly because of automation.

Sources: U.S. Census, Historical Statistics of the United States, Series D 233–682; J. Bessen (2016), "How Computer Automation Affects Occupations".
