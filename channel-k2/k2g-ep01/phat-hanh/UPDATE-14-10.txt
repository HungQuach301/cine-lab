Updates (14 Oct 2026): The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security published guidance to U.S. Customs and Border Protection on how to assess "U.S. content" in products of Canada and Mexico that qualify for USMCA preferential treatment, for applying the metal tariffs under Proclamation 11032 of June 1, 2026 (Federal Register document 2026-20938: https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-20938). The notice implements that June 2026 proclamation; it does not set new tariff rates.
