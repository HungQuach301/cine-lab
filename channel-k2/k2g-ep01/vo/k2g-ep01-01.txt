This video explains how the system works. It isn't financial or investment advice. This story has three layers. The old one: how steelmaking grew in one river valley in Pennsylvania. The recent one: how governments, from Washington to Brussels, started putting hard limits on steel imports — in Washington's case, on national security grounds. And the new one: a price now attached to steel crossing one major border — the price of its carbon. Under all three, our read is that one mechanism is at work: control of the path from raw material to market — and, now, the tolls charged along it.
