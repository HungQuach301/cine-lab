GIAY PHEP: Wikimedia Commons - anh hien dai (kiem vat lieu/mau) cho cac HERO, kiem TUNG tep
Ngay doc: 2026-10-16 (phien phu Tai lieu dung HERO)
Nguoi giu: tung tac gia (ghi theo tep); nen tang Wikimedia Commons
Cach doc: API commons.wikimedia.org action=query&prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|size|sha1|extmetadata (truong LicenseShortName, LicenseUrl, Artist, DateTimeOriginal, AttributionRequired), nguyen van gia tri tra ve.
TINH TRANG TAI: API va upload.wikimedia.org tra HTTP 429 ("You are making too many requests to the API") lien tuc trong phien -> CHUA tai tep anh; SHA-256 chua co. Moi tep duoi co SHA-1 do Commons cong bo de kiem khi tai sau.
Luat ap dung: chi PD / CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA; KHONG NC/ND. CC BY-SA: ghi cong + share-alike neu sua doi chinh anh (dung lam tham chieu dung hinh 3D, khong dua anh vao khung hinh, thi khong phat sinh tac pham phai sinh cua anh).
Ghi chu tu do toan canh: anh chup cong trinh tu khong gian cong cong; quyen kien truc khong thuoc pham vi tep nay.
