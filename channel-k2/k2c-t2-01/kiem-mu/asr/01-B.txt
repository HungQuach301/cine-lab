In June 1942, a Liberty ship took 122 days on average. By December 1943, 39. As a publicity stunt, one Richmond yard built the Robert E. Peary in four days and 15 and a half hours. In 1943, a government photographer pictured one of the people building Liberty ships in Richmond. Josie Lucille Owens, a welder trainee, helping build the Liberty ship George Washington Carver. The four Richmond yards produced 747 ships, more than any other shipyard complex in the country. Richmond grew from fewer than 24,000 people in 1940 to nearly 100,000 by 1943.
