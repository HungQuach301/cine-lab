In 1943, at the peak, America launched three cargo ships a day. In a 2026 report, Congress's researchers put America's share of world shipbuilding tonnage at 0.04 percent. Our read of the record: America didn't forget how to build ships. Since the 1980s, it has built few because few orders came — and the record ties high prices to fewer domestic purchases, and the lack of exports to missing economies of scale.
