At the time of its completion, Hoover Dam was the highest concrete arch dam in the world. By 1940 the dam's power plant was the largest hydroelectric generating plant in the world in terms of installed capacity. Hoover Dam allowed massive irrigation and municipal water systems to be built in California and Arizona, and electricity generated at the dam's power plant supported WWII war industries and post-war development in California, Arizona, and Nevada...
