## HỒI 1 — CHÁY 2–6/9/1666 (0:43–4:00 · 27 %)

**L08** Sunday, the second of September 1666. At about one in the morning, fire breaks out in Pudding Lane, in the house of Thomas Farriner ⟦THẺ⟧, baker to the King.
> claim: ≈ 1 giờ sáng CN 2/9, Pudding Lane, nhà thợ bánh của Vua · nguồn: Relation d.16 "about one a clock in the morning … in Pudding-Lane"; Pepys d.1082 "the King's baker's' house in Pudding-lane"; themonument.org.uk/history (N1, tên Farriner) · SC + TC + ĐK (6)

**L09** London is a city of timber. Its houses are packed so tight that their upper storeys almost touch across the lanes.
> claim: nhà gỗ, tầng trên chìa sát nhau · nguồn: City of London, *Great Fire walk* PDF, mở đầu (N3) · TC

**L10** Nineteen years earlier, the engraver Wenceslaus Hollar ⟦THẺ⟧ had published a long view of this skyline, drawn from St Saviour's church, just along the bank: a forest of church towers, and above them all, the long roof of Old St Paul's.
> claim: Hollar 'Long View' xuất bản 1647, vẽ từ St Saviour's (nay Southwark Cathedral) · nguồn: Folgerpedia (N22); ĐK (12) · TC + ĐK · hình: tranh khắc chồng cảnh — Hollar Long View là rủi ro VỪA (chưa xác nhận bản scan PD) → dùng Met 56.581.8 / 360649 (CC0) cho cùng ý nếu chưa xác nhận

**L11** The summer has been dry, and a fierce east wind is blowing.
> claim: mùa khô, gió đông mạnh · nguồn: Evelyn d.959 "a fierce eastern wind in a very dry season"; Relation d.16 "a violent Easterly wind" · SC

**L12** By morning, the fire has taken the church of St Magnus and most of Fish Street, at the foot of London Bridge.
> claim: sáng 2/9 St Magnus + phần lớn Fish Street cháy · nguồn: Pepys d.1082 · SC


**L13** He hurries to Whitehall and tells the King that unless houses are pulled down, nothing can stop the fire. The King sends him to the Lord Mayor with that order.
> claim: Pepys báo Vua; Vua sai Pepys tới Thị trưởng · nguồn: Pepys d.1104–1110 · SC

**L14** The Lord Mayor, Sir Thomas Bludworth ⟦THẺ⟧, is overwhelmed. Samuel Pepys ⟦THẺ⟧ finds him in Cannon Street, a handkerchief round his neck: "Lord, what can I do? I am spent. People will not obey me."
> claim: lời Bludworth với Pepys · nguồn: Pepys d.1129–1131 (nguyên văn: "Lord! what can I do? I am spent: people will not obey me.") · SC · **KHÔNG** dùng câu "a woman might piss it out" (không có trong Pepys — N2)

**L15** "I have been pulling down houses, but the fire overtakes us faster than we can do it."
> claim: tiếp lời Bludworth · nguồn: Pepys d.1131–1132 · SC

**L16** London Bridge survives. A gap left by an earlier fire, in 1633, stops the flames from crossing to Southwark.
> claim: khoảng trống do cháy 1633 chặn lửa sang Southwark · nguồn: Relation d.15 (cháy 13/2/1633 "from S. Magnus to the first open place"); City of London walk PDF "London Bridge" (N4) · SC + TC

**L17** Instead, the wind drives the fire west, along the waterfront, and up the hill.
> claim: lửa lan tây dọc bờ sông (tới Steelyard trưa 2/9) và lên đồi · nguồn: Pepys d.1087 (Steelyard), d.1200 ("in a bow up the hill") · SC

**L18** That evening, Pepys takes shelter in a little alehouse on Bankside — about where we are standing.
> claim: Pepys ở quán bia trên Bankside, đối diện Three Cranes · nguồn: Pepys d.1193–1194 "a little ale-house on the Bankside, over against the 'Three Cranes" · SC · "about where we are standing" = điểm máy Bankside (bậc 1 vị trí Three Cranes ở Vintry, ĐK T4-G3)

**L19** As it grows dark, he sees "one entire arch of fire" — more than a mile long. "It made me weep to see it."
> claim: vòng cung lửa > 1 dặm · nguồn: Pepys d.1200–1202 · SC


**L20** Monday. The fire spreads up Gracechurch Street to Lombard Street, and west to Cannon Street.
> claim: sáng thứ Hai tới Gracechurch St, Lombard St, St Swithin's (Cannon St) · nguồn: Relation d.16 · SC

**L21** At two in the afternoon, it takes the Royal Exchange — the City's trading floor.
> claim: Royal Exchange cháy thứ Hai 14:00 · nguồn: City of London walk PDF "The Royal Exchange" (N5); Pepys d.1365 (phế tích 5/9) · TC + SC · "trading floor": diễn đạt — **[cần kiểm cách nói]** (Exchange = nơi buôn bán của thương nhân)

**L22** The roads are jammed with carts. The river fills with boats — and with goods floating on the water.
> claim: phố/đường chật xe; sông đầy thuyền, đồ trôi · nguồn: Pepys d.1156, d.1174; Evelyn d.977 "the Thames covered with goods floating" · SC

**L23** John Evelyn ⟦THẺ⟧ walks the burning streets that night. The sky, he writes, is "like the top of a burning oven"; the noise is "like a hideous storm" — the cracking of the flames, the fall of towers, houses and churches.
> claim: trời như miệng lò, tiếng như bão · nguồn: Evelyn d.985, d.988–990 (mục 3/9) · SC · (tiếng động thời kỳ, nâng cấp #5)

**L24** Tuesday, the fourth. The fire climbs Ludgate Hill to St Paul's Cathedral, wrapped in wooden scaffolding for repairs.

**L25** By evening, the great church is ablaze. John Evelyn ⟦THẺ⟧ watches its lead run down the streets "in a stream".
> claim: St Paul's cháy tối 4/9; chì chảy thành dòng · nguồn: Pepys d.1317 ("Paul's is burned"); Evelyn d.1005 "the melting lead running down the streets in a stream" · SC

**L26** In the west, the fire crosses the Fleet and runs down Fleet Street, as far as the Temple.
> claim: tới Fleet St, Inner Temple, St Dunstan's · nguồn: Evelyn d.1001; Relation d.16 ("to S. Dunstan's Church in Fleetstreet") · SC

**L27** Only now are houses blown up with gunpowder to open firebreaks — first in Tower Street, near the Tower.
> claim: nổ nhà bằng thuốc súng từ thứ Ba 4/9, Tower Street · nguồn: Pepys d.1287 "Now begins the practice of blowing up of houses in Tower-streete"; Monument CMP §2.1 (N7) · SC + TC

**L28** At the Navy Office, Pepys and a colleague dig a pit in the garden for their wine — and Pepys buries his Parmesan cheese.
> claim: đào hố chôn rượu + phô mai Parmesan · nguồn: Pepys d.1262–1265 (mục 4/9) · SC

**L29** That night, the wind finally drops.
> claim: gió dịu tối thứ Ba 4/9 · nguồn: Relation d.16 ("at 10 of the clock on Tuesday-night … the wind slacked"); Monument CMP (N7) · SC + TC

**L30** By Thursday, the fire is out.
> claim: khống chế 5–6/9 · nguồn: ĐK (6); Relation d.16 (bùng lại ở Inner Temple đêm thứ Tư, dập trước sáng) · ĐK + SC

**L31** Homeless Londoners camp in the fields outside the walls — Moorfields, St George's Fields, as far as Highgate — "some under tents, some under miserable huts and hovels."
> claim: dân tản cư ở các cánh đồng · nguồn: Evelyn d.1049–1051 (mục 5/9) · SC

**L32** About thirteen thousand two hundred houses. Eighty-seven parish churches. St Paul's. Roughly four-fifths of the City — over four hundred acres.

**L33** Fewer than ten deaths were recorded. The real toll is unknown.
> claim: cách nói T4-G2 · nguồn: ĐK (6) · ĐK

## HỒI 2 — LÀM LẠI LẦN 1, 1666–1711 (4:00–6:38 · 22 %)

**L34** The ashes are still warm when the plans arrive.

**L38** Wren's plan survives only on paper — engraved and sold a century later as the London that might have been.
> claim: bản khắc phương án Wren lưu hành thế kỷ 18 (LoC 2004678686, 'A Plan for rebuilding the City of London…', 1767, 'unhappily defeated by Faction') · nguồn: loc.gov/pictures/item/2004678686 (đọc 23/10/2026; rights: 'No known restrictions on publication') · SC (bản in) · tư liệu chồng cảnh: có

**L40** So Parliament moves faster. From late February 1667, a special Fire Court sits at Clifford's Inn, settling disputes between landlords and tenants. In its first year, it decides three hundred and seventy-four cases.
> claim: Toà Cháy theo Fire of London Disputes Act 1666; ngồi ở Clifford's Inn từ cuối 2/1667; 374 vụ năm đầu · nguồn: ĐK (2); Inner Temple Library *Great Fire* 2016 (N25, N11) · ĐK + TC · cách nói "late February 1667" (T4-G2)

**L41** A Rebuilding Act sets the rules: outside walls of brick or stone, not timber. Four classes of house — two storeys on the by-lanes, three on streets of note, four on the high streets, and four for mansions of the greatest size.

**L43** The old street plan stays. Two new streets — King Street and Queen Street — are cut down from the Guildhall toward the river.
> claim: King St, Queen St mới nối Guildhall ra sông · nguồn: London Museum object 466275 (N10) · TC

**L44** Rebuilding gets going in 1668. By the end of 1670, about six thousand new houses are up — on the same lanes, in brick.

**L45** City surveyors — Hooke among them — set out the ground, plot by plot.

**L47** Fire insurance begins here too. In 1680, Nicholas Barbon ⟦THẺ⟧ opens the Fire Office.
