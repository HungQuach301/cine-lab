This is a frame from our own 3D reconstruction of London in the year 1711, seen from the tower of St Saviour's church on the south bank, about 125 m high, looking north-west across the river to the rebuilt brick City. Repaint ONLY the SURFACES of the walls and roofs of the ordinary houses so they look like a real photograph of the year 1711: new red and brown brick walls with white-painted timber sash and casement windows, old clay-tile and grey slate roofs with a little soot, each house a little different. Keep every building exactly where it is, with exactly the same outline, height, roof shape and the same number of buildings: do not add, remove, merge, split or move any building. Do not add, remove or change any roof, gable, chimney, chimney pot, dormer, storey, cornice, balcony, bay or porch: the chimneys already in the image are the only chimneys. Do not add architectural details that are not in the image (no new cornices, string courses, quoins, pediments, brackets, extra windows or doors); keep every window and door opening where it is. Change only colour, material texture, weathering and dirt. Do not add anything: no people, animals, vehicles, boats, carts, smoke, fire, flags, signs, lettering, lamps, street furniture, scaffolding, trees or plants. Do not paint any reflections on water or glass. Do not change the river, the river banks, the sky, the cathedral, the churches, the towers or any bridge. Nothing from after 1711.
