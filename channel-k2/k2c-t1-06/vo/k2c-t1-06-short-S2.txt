In the model code's 2021 edition — as reprinted in Seattle's 2021 code, which we checked — an apartment building may have a single staircase up to the third story, with at most four homes per floor and sprinklers throughout. A fourth story on one stair is not permitted. So the single-stair limit bites at the fourth story, and at any floor with more than four homes — the taller building on a small lot.
