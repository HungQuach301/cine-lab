S1 — 1990: "more are certain to disappear"
In 1990 the Bureau of Labor Statistics wrote that newspaper equipment had already eliminated many typesetting jobs, and more were certain to disappear. The work did not vanish: designers began doing it themselves, on computers. Full film: "The Computer Replaced the Typesetter and Made the Designer. Now AI Can Draw" on the channel.
Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook 1990–91. #history #design #AI

S2 — Image work posts −17% after image AI
One study of a large online freelancing platform found that posts for graphic design and 3D modeling work fell about 17% within a year of image generators, compared with manual jobs. One platform; job posts, not jobs. Full film on the channel.
Source: Demirci, Hannane & Zhu, CESifo Working Paper 11276 (2024). #design #AI

S3 — BLS: AI will reduce the need for graphic designers
The BLS projects that automated design tools, such as AI, will make graphic designers more productive and reduce the need for these workers: a projected decline of 1.7% from 2025 to 2035, while all jobs are projected to grow 3.5%. A projection, not a count. Full film on the channel.
Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook (2025–35). #jobs #design #AI
